# 10 a second, never back to the sender (the socket task sends them to the others).
> {"event":"pusher:subscribe","data":{"channel":"private-chat.1","auth":"{auth:private-chat.1}"}}

< {"event":"pusher_internal:subscription_succeeded","channel":"private-chat.1","data":{}}
